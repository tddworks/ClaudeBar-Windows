ClaudeBar for Windows · Leaderboard

A Windows 11 app, Fluent and native: Mica window, Segoe UI Variable, Windows' own accent and settings cards. ClaudeBar comes through in what the Mac's Leaderboard already has: one big rank, each provider in its own colour, the podium, and exactly what gets uploaded. The window is the main surface; the taskbar button gives the glance (hover card, right-click menu), and a tray icon is an option for people who want one. Sample data only.

Notes

Against the build in #4 today

  • A designed window in place of stacked default controls: custom title bar, a header with Refresh and Settings, a status bar for uploads.
  • Your rank gets the provider mix and the gap; the board gets the podium, avatars, mix bars and provider pills.
  • Settings move from a tab to the gear, as Windows Settings-style cards; Export and the taskbar and tray options join them.
  • Join shows exactly what gets uploaded, and greys out a provider with no tokens on this PC.

In WinUI 3

  • Window: ExtendsContentIntoTitleBar, Mica, SelectorBar for the period, ToggleButton pills, ItemsRepeater for the board.
  • Settings cards: hand-built to the Windows Settings layout, or CommunityToolkit's SettingsCard.
  • Mix bars: each member's byProvider, which /board already sends; the C interface passes it on.
  • Hover card: DwmSetIconicThumbnail; jump list: JumpList; badge: BadgeUpdateManager (MSIX only); tray: Shell_NotifyIcon; toast: App Notifications.
  • Light, dark and high contrast follow Windows; the accent is Windows' own.

Not in this version

  • Share my rank as an image, the globe and profile links: the Mac has them; later here.
  • Devices (add this PC to a member, the devices list): after the Mac's slices 14 and 15.
  • The badge needs MSIX; without it the hover card and jump list still work.
  • Avatars are initials on a colour from the name; no pictures are uploaded.
